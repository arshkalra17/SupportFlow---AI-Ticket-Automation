# Electronics Care and Troubleshooting

## Device will not turn on
Charge the device for at least 30 minutes with the original cable, then hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a restart. If it still does not start, the fault may be covered by the warranty.

## Battery draining quickly
Update the device software, lower screen brightness, and turn off location services for apps that do not need it. Battery capacity naturally falls over time and battery wear is not a warranty fault.

## Headphones not connecting
Remove the headphones from your device's Bluetooth list, put them back in pairing mode by holding the power button for 7 seconds, and pair again. Only one source device can be connected at a time.

## Keyboard keys not responding
For wireless keyboards, replace the batteries or recharge first. Debris under keys can be removed with compressed air; do not prise keys off, as that is not covered by the warranty.

## Monitor shows no signal
Check that the cable is firmly connected at both ends and that the correct input is selected in the monitor's menu. Try a different cable before requesting a replacement.
