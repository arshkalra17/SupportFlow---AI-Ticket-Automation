# Pre-orders and Backorders

## How pre-orders work
Pre-ordered items are reserved for you and ship on or shortly after the release date shown on the product page. Your card is authorised when you pre-order and charged when the item ships.

## Pre-order price guarantee
If the price of a pre-ordered item goes down before it ships, you automatically pay the lower price. If it goes up, you keep the price you pre-ordered at.

## Backordered items
A backordered item is temporarily out of stock. The order stays in PROCESSING until stock arrives, and you can cancel it at any time while it is still PROCESSING.

## Mixed orders
If an order contains both in-stock and pre-order items, the in-stock items ship first at no extra shipping cost, and the pre-order items ship separately when available.

## Release date delays
If a release date moves by more than 30 days, we email you and give you the option to keep or cancel the pre-order.
