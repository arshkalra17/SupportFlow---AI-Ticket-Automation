# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and SupportFlow gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in several parcels, you may see one charge per parcel.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
A pending authorisation and a final charge can briefly look like two charges; the authorisation disappears within 3-5 business days. If you see two final charges for the same order, contact support immediately and we will investigate as a priority.

## Invoices and receipts
A receipt is emailed when your order ships. VAT invoices for business customers can be requested from support and are issued within 2 business days.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order stays in PROCESSING for 48 hours while you update your payment details. After that it is cancelled automatically and any authorisation is released.
