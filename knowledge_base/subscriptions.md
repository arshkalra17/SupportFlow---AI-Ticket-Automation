# Subscription Orders

## How subscriptions work
Consumables such as printer ink, filters and cleaning supplies can be ordered on a subscription that delivers every 1, 2 or 3 months. Subscribers get 5% off every delivery and free standard shipping.

## Changing or skipping a delivery
You can change the frequency, the quantity, or skip the next delivery from your account settings up to 3 days before the next delivery is created. After that, the delivery is already in PROCESSING.

## Cancelling a subscription
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with no fee. Cancelling stops future deliveries; a delivery that has already been created follows the normal order cancellation rules.

## Price changes
If the price of a subscribed item changes, we email you at least 7 days before the next delivery that uses the new price.

## Payment failures on subscriptions
If a subscription payment fails, we retry once after 2 days. If it fails again, that delivery is skipped and the subscription stays active for the next cycle.
