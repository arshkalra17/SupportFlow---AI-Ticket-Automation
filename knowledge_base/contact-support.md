# Contacting Support and Escalations

## Support hours
The support assistant is available 24 hours a day. Human agents are available Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern Time, and Saturday 9am to 5pm. Requests made outside these hours are answered the next working day.

## Response times
Chat replies from a human agent usually arrive within 10 minutes during opening hours. Support tickets are answered within 1 business day; urgent tickets about fraud or duplicate charges are prioritised.

## Talking to a human
Ask the assistant to connect you with a person at any time. It opens a support ticket with your conversation attached so you do not have to repeat yourself.

## Escalating a complaint
If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, ask for an escalation. A team lead reviews the case within 2 business days and contacts you directly.

## Checking on a support ticket
You can ask the assistant for the status of any of your support tickets. Each ticket has a number and a status: open, in progress, waiting for customer, or resolved.
