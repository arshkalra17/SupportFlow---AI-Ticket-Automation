# Packaging and Recycling

## Our packaging
Most orders ship in recycled cardboard with paper padding. Fragile items such as monitors are packed with moulded pulp inserts instead of foam.

## Recycling old electronics
We take back old electronics of any brand for free recycling. Request a prepaid recycling label from support; devices are wiped and either refurbished or recycled by a certified partner.

## Battery disposal
Batteries must not go in household waste. Tape the terminals of lithium batteries and drop them at a battery collection point, or send them back with our recycling label.

## Reducing packaging
At checkout you can choose "ship in fewest boxes", which may delay delivery by a day but combines items into fewer parcels.

## Returning packaging
We do not accept returned packaging on its own, but you can reuse our boxes when sending a return.
