# Accessibility

## Website and app accessibility
Our website and app aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. They work with common screen readers, support keyboard-only navigation and respect your device's text-size settings.

## Alternative formats
Product manuals and receipts are available in large print or as accessible PDFs on request through support.

## Delivery assistance
Customers who need help receiving deliveries can add delivery instructions, such as leaving parcels at a specific door or allowing extra time to answer, in their account settings.

## Accessible communication
You can contact support by chat, email or text relay service. If you need more time in a conversation, the assistant will not close the conversation while you are still replying.

## Reporting an accessibility problem
If something on our site does not work with your assistive technology, tell support. Accessibility bugs are reported directly to our product team and we reply with an update within 10 business days.
