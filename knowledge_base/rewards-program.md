# Rewards Program

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent on full-priced items. Points are added when the order is delivered, not when it is placed. Shipping costs, taxes and gift card purchases do not earn points.

## Redeeming points
Every 100 points are worth $5 off a future order. Points can be combined with a promo code and can cover up to 50% of an order's value.

## Points expiry
Points expire 12 months after they were earned if you have not placed any order in that time. Any purchase resets the 12-month clock for all your points.

## Returns and points
If you return an item, the points earned on it are removed. If points were used to pay for a returned item, they are added back to your balance rather than refunded as money.

## Membership tiers
Members who spend more than $1000 in a calendar year reach Gold tier, which adds free express shipping and early access to sales for the following year.
