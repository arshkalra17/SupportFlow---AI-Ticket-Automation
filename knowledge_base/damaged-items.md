# Damaged or Faulty Items on Arrival

## What counts as damaged on arrival
An item is damaged on arrival if it is broken, cracked, dented or does not work when you first unpack it. Report it within 7 days of delivery so we can file a claim with the carrier.

## Your options
You can choose a replacement under our Item Replacement Policy or a refund under our Return and Refund Policy. Damaged-on-arrival refunds do not require you to send the item back first.

## Evidence we ask for
Photos of the item, the damage, and the outer box with the shipping label. Keep the packaging until your request is resolved, as the carrier may want to inspect it.

## Faults that appear later
If an item worked at first and developed a fault after the first 30 days, it is handled as a warranty claim rather than as damage on arrival.

## Partial damage in multi-item orders
If only some items in an order arrived damaged, the request covers just those items; the rest of the order is not affected.
