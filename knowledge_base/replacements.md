# Item Replacement Policy

## When a replacement is available
Replacements can be requested for orders that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. If you received the wrong colour, size or model, that counts as an incorrect item.

## Eligible order statuses
Orders in PROCESSING, SHIPPED, or DELIVERED status are eligible for replacement requests. Cancelled orders are not eligible, because nothing was shipped.

## What we need from you
Tell us the order number and what is wrong with the item. For damaged items, a photo of the damage and of the outer packaging helps us resolve the request faster and lets us file a claim with the carrier.

## What happens next
A replacement request is reviewed and, once accepted, the new item ships with the same speed as your original order at no extra cost. You will receive a prepaid label to send the original item back within 14 days. If the item is out of stock, we offer a refund instead.

## Replacement or refund?
You can choose a refund instead of a replacement if you are still within the 30-day refund window. Replacements for defective items after the refund window has closed are covered by the product warranty instead.
