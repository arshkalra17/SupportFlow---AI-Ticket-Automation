# Product Warranty

## Warranty length
Electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. Accessories such as cases, cables and chargers have a 90-day warranty. Extended protection plans add 2 more years.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal use: a device that stops charging, dead pixels on a screen, buttons that stop responding. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, normal wear such as scratches, or damage from unauthorised repairs.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with the order number and a description of the fault. We may ask for a short video of the problem. Approved claims are repaired or replaced; if neither is possible, we refund the original purchase price.

## Warranty versus returns
Within the first 30 days after delivery, a faulty item can simply be refunded or replaced under our return and replacement policies, which is usually faster. After 30 days, faults are handled as warranty claims.

## Refurbished products
Refurbished items carry a 6-month warranty and are tested to the same standard as new products.
