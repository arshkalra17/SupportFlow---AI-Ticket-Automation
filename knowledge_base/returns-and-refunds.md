# Return and Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility window
Customers may request a refund for eligible orders within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your order history, not from the date you placed the order. Orders that were never delivered are handled under our lost and delayed package process instead.

## Items that cannot be refunded
Gift cards, downloadable software that has been activated, and personalised or engraved items cannot be refunded unless they arrived damaged or defective. Hygiene products such as earbuds and headphone tips can only be refunded if the original seal is unbroken.

## How refund approval works
Refunds of $1000 or less are auto-approved by the system as soon as the request passes our checks. Refunds exceeding $1000 require human admin approval before execution; a member of our team reviews these, usually within one business day, and you will be notified of the decision in your conversation. A refund can never be larger than the amount paid for the order.

## How you receive your money
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear within 5-7 business days after approval, depending on your bank. Orders paid with a gift card are refunded to a new gift card. We do not refund to a different card or to a bank account.

## Returning the item
For most refunds we ask you to send the item back. Once the refund request is created you will receive a return label by email; parcels must be dropped off within 14 days. Refunds for items that are damaged on arrival do not require a return.
