# Shipping & Delivery Guidelines

## Delivery speeds
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after 2pm local time are processed the next business day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50; below that it costs $4.99. Express shipping costs $14.99 regardless of order value. Large items such as monitors and furniture may carry an oversize surcharge, shown at checkout.

## Tracking numbers
Tracking numbers are generated once an order status updates to SHIPPED. You will receive the tracking number by email, and it also appears in your order details.

## Delivery areas
We deliver to all 50 US states. Deliveries to PO boxes are only possible with standard shipping. Express shipping is not available to Alaska, Hawaii or US territories.

## Signature on delivery
Orders above $500 require a signature on delivery. If nobody is available, the carrier leaves a notice and tries again the next business day, up to three attempts, before returning the parcel to us.
