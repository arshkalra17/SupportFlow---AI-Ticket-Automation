# Gift Cards

## Buying gift cards
Digital gift cards are available from $10 to $500 and are emailed to the recipient on the date you choose. Physical gift cards ship with standard shipping only.

## Balance and expiry
SupportFlow gift cards do not expire and carry no fees. You can check the balance in your account or at checkout.

## Lost or stolen gift cards
We can replace a lost digital gift card if you can show the original purchase receipt and the card has not been redeemed. Physical cards that are lost or stolen cannot be replaced, so treat them like cash.

## Refunds of gift cards
Gift cards cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash, except where required by law. Orders paid with a gift card are refunded to a new gift card for the same amount.

## Gift card scams
We will never ask you to pay a bill, a fine or a support fee with a gift card. If someone asks you to, it is a scam; do not share the card number and report it to us.
