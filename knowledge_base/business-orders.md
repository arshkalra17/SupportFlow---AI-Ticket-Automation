# Business and Bulk Orders

## Business accounts
Registered businesses can open a business account to get VAT invoices, centralised billing and multiple users on one account. Applications are reviewed within 3 business days.

## Bulk pricing
Orders of 20 or more units of the same item qualify for tiered bulk pricing. Request a quote through support; quotes are valid for 30 days.

## Paying by invoice
Approved business accounts can pay by invoice with 30-day payment terms. Orders above the account's credit limit need a manager's approval on our side before they ship.

## Returns for business orders
Bulk orders follow the standard 30-day return policy, but returns of more than 10 units need to be arranged with support first so we can schedule a pallet collection.

## Tax-exempt purchases
Tax-exempt organisations can upload their exemption certificate in the business account settings. Tax is removed from future orders once the certificate is verified.
