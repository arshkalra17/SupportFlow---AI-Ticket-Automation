# Price Adjustments and Price Matching

## Price drops after you buy
If the price of an item drops within 14 days of your order being delivered, we refund the difference once. Ask support with your order number; clearance sales and flash deals are excluded.

## Price matching competitors
We match the price of major US retailers for an identical item in stock, sold and shipped by the retailer itself. Marketplace sellers, auction sites and membership-only prices are not matched. The match must be requested before you place the order.

## Bundles and multi-buy offers
Bundle prices apply only when all items in the bundle are bought together. If you return one item from a bundle, the refund is adjusted so that the remaining items are charged at their individual prices.

## Price errors
Occasionally an item is listed at an obviously wrong price. If an order is placed at a mistaken price we may cancel it while it is still in PROCESSING and refund the full amount.

## Currency
All prices are in US dollars. Orders from international customers are charged in USD; your bank may apply its own exchange rate and fees.
