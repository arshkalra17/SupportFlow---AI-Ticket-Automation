# Tracking Your Order

## Order statuses explained
PROCESSING means we have your order and are preparing it; it can still be cancelled. SHIPPED means it has left our warehouse with a carrier. DELIVERED means the carrier has confirmed delivery. CANCELLED means the order was stopped before shipping and will not arrive.

## Where to find tracking
The tracking number appears in your order details once the status is SHIPPED, and the support assistant can look it up for you if you give it the order number.

## Tracking not updating
Carriers often take 24-48 hours to show the first scan after a label is created. If tracking has not changed for more than 5 business days, ask support to open an investigation with the carrier.

## Delivery estimates
The estimated delivery date is calculated from the shipping speed you chose and the date the order shipped. Estimates are not guarantees; weather and carrier delays can push them back.

## Split shipments
Orders with items from different warehouses may arrive in more than one parcel, each with its own tracking number. Your order shows as DELIVERED only when every parcel has arrived.
