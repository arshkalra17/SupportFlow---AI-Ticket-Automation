# International Shipping

## Countries we ship to
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and Japan. Orders to other countries cannot be placed online; contact support for a quote on large business orders.

## International delivery times
International standard delivery takes 7-14 business days. International express takes 3-5 business days. Delivery times exclude time spent in customs.

## Customs, duties and import taxes
Prices on our site do not include import duties or local taxes. These are collected by the carrier on delivery and are the customer's responsibility. If you refuse to pay them, the parcel is returned to us and we refund the order minus the original shipping cost.

## Restricted items
Lithium batteries sold separately, aerosols and some chargers cannot be shipped internationally. These items are removed from the basket automatically when you enter an international address.

## International returns
International customers can return items within the same 30-day window, but return shipping is not prepaid. We recommend a tracked service, since we cannot refund parcels that never reach us.
