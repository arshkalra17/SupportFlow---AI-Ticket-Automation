# Lost, Stolen or Delayed Packages

## Package marked delivered but not received
First check around your property, with neighbours, and in any parcel locker or reception desk. Carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered up to 24 hours before they actually arrive. If it still has not turned up after 48 hours, contact support with your order number.

## Investigating a lost parcel
We open a trace with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. Missing parcels are not counted against the 30-day refund window.

## Stolen packages
If you believe a parcel was stolen after delivery, file a police report and send us the reference number. We will reship the order once per customer per year for theft claims; beyond that, please contact your home insurer.

## Damaged in transit
Parcels that arrive visibly crushed or wet should be photographed before opening. Damaged items are handled through our replacement policy, and you do not need to return a damaged item until we ask for it.

## Delayed packages
If a parcel is more than 5 business days past its estimated delivery date, we refund the shipping fee for express orders automatically. Standard shipping fees are not refunded for delays.
