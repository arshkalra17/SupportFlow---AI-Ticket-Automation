# Account and Login Security

## Resetting your password
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you use the address the account was created with.

## Two-step verification
You can turn on two-step verification in your account settings using an authenticator app. Once enabled, every new device needs a six-digit code. Keep your backup codes somewhere safe; support cannot bypass two-step verification without identity checks.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. If it stays locked, contact support; we will verify your identity before unlocking it.

## Suspicious activity
If you see orders you did not place or your email or password was changed without you, reset your password immediately and contact support. We can freeze the account and cancel any PROCESSING orders while we investigate.

## Support will never ask for your password
Our support assistant and agents never ask for your password, full card number or two-step codes. Identity is handled by your signed-in session.
