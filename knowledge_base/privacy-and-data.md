# Privacy and Your Data

## What we collect
We store your contact details, delivery addresses, order history and support conversations. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

## How support conversations are used
Conversations with the support assistant are stored to provide context for follow-up questions and are reviewed in aggregate to improve the service. They are kept for 24 months.

## Downloading your data
You can request a copy of your personal data from your account settings. The export is prepared within 30 days and sent to your account email as a download link.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion from your account settings. Deletion is completed within 30 days. We keep order and invoice records for 7 years where tax law requires it, but they are no longer linked to an active account.

## Marketing emails
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email. Order confirmations, shipping updates and support replies are service messages and are always sent.
